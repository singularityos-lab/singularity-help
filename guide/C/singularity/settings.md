---
title: Settings
description: Change how the desktop looks and works.
---

Open **Settings** from the launcher, or click the status icons in the top bar and choose the settings button.

- **Desktop**: wallpaper, accent color, light or dark style, the dock and the top bar.
- **Displays**: resolution, scale, arrangement of several monitors.
- **Keyboard**: layouts, shortcuts and touchpad gestures.
- **Sound**: output and input devices, and the volume of each app.
- **Users**: accounts, and how the login screen looks.
- **Accessibility**: large text, high contrast, mono audio and more.
- **Performance**: power profiles, and what happens when you close the laptop lid.

> Apps that have their own options appear in **Settings > Apps**.
