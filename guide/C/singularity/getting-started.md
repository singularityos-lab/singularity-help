## The top bar

The bar at the top of the screen shows the menus of the app you are using, the clock, and the status of the network, sound and battery. Click the clock to see the calendar, the bell to see your notifications, and the status icons for quick settings.

## The dock

The dock at the bottom holds your favorite apps and the apps that are open. A dot under an icon means the app is running. Right-click an icon to open a new window, to keep the app in the dock, or to quit it.

## Opening apps

1. Press <kbd>Super</kbd> or <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> to open the launcher.
2. Start typing the name of an app, a file or a setting.
3. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open the first result.

> The launcher can also do quick math: type `12*7` and the result appears right away.

## Finding anything quickly

Press <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> to open the command palette. It runs commands and actions without opening any menu.
