---
title: Windows
description: Snap, arrange and close windows.
---

## Snapping

- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Left</kbd> or <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Right</kbd> fills the left or right half of the screen.
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Up</kbd> fills the top half, and pressed again fills the whole screen.
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Down</kbd> fills the bottom half.

## Arranging all windows

Press <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd> to arrange every window of the current workspace in a grid.

## Picture in picture

Keep an eye on a video or a window while you work:

- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd> shows the focused window in a small floating view.
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd> lets you pick a region of a window to show.

## Closing windows from the overview

In the workspace overview, click a window with the middle mouse button to close it without leaving the overview.

## Seeing the desktop

Press <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> to move the windows aside and see the desktop. Press it again to bring them back.
