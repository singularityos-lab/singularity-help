---
title: Wi-Fi and Networks
description: Connect to a wireless network, and what to try when the connection does not work.
---

Click the status icons in the top bar to open the quick settings. The **Wi-Fi** tile turns wireless on and off; open its page to see the networks nearby and click one to connect. Networks that need a password ask for it the first time.

You find every network option, wired connections and VPNs included, in **Settings > Network**.

## When Wi-Fi does not connect

- Check that **Airplane Mode** is off in the quick settings.
- Turn **Wi-Fi** off and on again, then pick the network once more.
- If the password was changed, choose the network in **Settings > Network**, forget it, and connect again with the new password.
- Move closer to the router, or restart the router if other devices cannot connect either.
- A **Wi-Fi Hotspot** uses the same adapter: turn it off to join a network.

> The **Logs** app shows the messages of the network service. Search for `log NetworkManager` in the launcher to open them.
