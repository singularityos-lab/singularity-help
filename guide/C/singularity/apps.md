---
title: Installing Apps
description: Find and install new apps with Store.
---

1. Open **Store** from the launcher or the dock.
2. Browse the featured apps and categories, or search for an app by name.
3. Open an app to see its screenshots, rating and the permissions it asks for.
4. Click **Get** to install it.

Installed apps appear in the launcher right away. Updates are listed under **Updates** in Store.

> The permissions list tells you what an app can reach, such as your files, the network or the camera. Read it before installing apps you do not know.
