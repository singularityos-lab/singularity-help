---
title: Screenshots
description: Capture the screen, a window or a region.
---

- <kbd>Print</kbd> opens the screenshot tool, where you choose what to capture.
- <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Print</kbd> lets you drag a rectangle over the part of the screen you want.
- <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Print</kbd> captures the window you are using.

Screenshots are saved in the **Screenshots** folder inside **Pictures**, and copied to the clipboard so you can paste them straight into a message or a document.
