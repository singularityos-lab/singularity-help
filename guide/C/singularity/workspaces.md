Workspaces are separate desktops, each with its own windows. Use them to keep tasks apart, for example mail on one and a project on another.

## The workspace overview

Press <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>W</kbd> to see all workspaces. From the overview you can:

- click a workspace to look at it, and double-click to go there;
- drag a window onto another workspace to move it;
- click the last, empty workspace to create a new one.

## Touchpad gestures

Swipe with several fingers on the touchpad:

- left or right to change workspace;
- down to open the workspace overview;
- up to open the launcher.

You can combine directions in one movement: swipe sideways to pick a workspace, then down to open its overview.

> Choose whether gestures use three or four fingers, and how sensitive they are, in **Settings > Keyboard**.
