---
title: Keyboard Shortcuts
description: The complete list of desktop shortcuts.
---

## Desktop

- <kbd>Super</kbd> or <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd>: open the launcher
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd>: open the command palette
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>W</kbd>: show the workspace overview
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd>: show the desktop
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Return</kbd>: open a terminal
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>.</kbd>: open the emoji picker
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>L</kbd>: lock the screen

## Windows

- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Left</kbd>, <kbd>Right</kbd>, <kbd>Up</kbd>, <kbd>Down</kbd>: snap the window
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd>: arrange windows in a grid
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd>: picture in picture of the window
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd>: picture in picture of a region

## Screenshots

- <kbd>Print</kbd>: screenshot tool
- <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Print</kbd>: capture a region
- <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Print</kbd>: capture the window

## Sound

- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>M</kbd>: mute or unmute

> You can change every shortcut in **Settings > Keyboard**.
